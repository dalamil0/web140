My Fitness Image Remix Gallery

Each fitness-inspired image below shows a different editing technique I experimented with in Canva.

01. The Classic — Black & White

For this image, I removed the color to create a classic black-and-white look. I liked how the edit puts more focus on the gym equipment, shadows, and details in the photo.

02. The Pop — Saturation

For this image, I increased the saturation to make the colors more vibrant and noticeable. I wanted the warm orange tones in the sunset to stand out and give the photo a brighter, more energetic feeling.

Original Image

Edited Image

03. The Drama — Contrast

For this image, I increased the contrast to create a stronger difference between the light and dark areas. This made the colors and details in the food stand out more and gave the image a bolder appearance.

Edited Image

04. The One-Color World — Monochromatic

For this image, I used different shades of blue to create a monochromatic effect. I chose blue because it gives the peaceful yoga scene a calm and focused feeling while still keeping the original details visible.

Icons That Represent My Theme

I selected these Font Awesome icons to represent the fitness, inspiration, and creativity behind my project.

Fitness

Fitness is a big part of my everyday life, so I wanted my theme to include images that represent strength, movement, nutrition, and wellness.

Inspiration

I chose images that reflect things I enjoy in my everyday life, especially fitness, food, and nature. I wanted each image to feel personal while still giving me the chance to experiment with different editing techniques.

Creativity

Editing gives me a way to take an ordinary image and make it feel completely different. I enjoyed experimenting with color, contrast, and different effects to see how small changes could completely change the mood of each photo.
