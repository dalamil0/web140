Part 6 • Explain Your Design Choices

My Color Analysis

Explain how your color choices relate to color theory and how you would use each palette in a website.

Palette 1

Harmony: Complementary

Why I selected these colors: I selected purple and yellow because they are complementary colors that create strong contrast while still making the design feel balanced.

Mood: This palette creates a creative, energetic, and welcoming mood. The purple feels bold while the yellow adds warmth and brightness.

Website Usage: I would use this palette for a creative website, portfolio, or brand that wants to feel modern and eye-catching.

Palette 2

Harmony: Analogous

Why I selected these colors: I selected pink, purple, and rose because they are close to each other on the color wheel and create a smooth, coordinated color scheme.

Mood: This palette creates a fun, creative, and energetic mood. The pink and rose make it feel warm while the purple adds a bold touch.

Website Usage: I would use this palette for a beauty, fashion, lifestyle, or creative website that wants to feel colorful and modern.

Palette 3

Harmony: Triadic

Why I selected these colors: I selected blue, red, and yellow because they form a triadic color scheme and provide strong contrast while keeping the design balanced.

Mood: This palette creates a bold, energetic, and playful mood. The three colors make the design feel bright and attention-grabbing.

Website Usage: I would use this palette for an educational, entertainment, or creative website that needs clear sections and eye catching colors.
